## About Me

- **Applied AI Engineer** building agentic AI, LLM and RAG systems for production
- **6+ years** of research and engineering experience, including **3+ years** shipping LLM systems for 10 clients across fintech, BFSI, insurance, e-commerce/D2C and B2B SaaS
- Client work has delivered **$1.2M+ in annualized impact** through support automation, less manual effort and higher conversion
- Builds multi-agent workflows with **LangGraph** and **LangChain**, retrieval pipelines on vector databases, and **FastAPI** services on **AWS** (Bedrock, ECS, Lambda)
- Tests every release with **RAGAS** and **LLM-as-a-Judge** evals, traced in **LangSmith** and **Langfuse**
- **Ph.D. in Physics** from IIT (ISM) Dhanbad, with 5 peer-reviewed papers on semiconductor gas and humidity sensors
- Ask me about **multi-agent systems, RAG, LLM evaluation, guardrails, and cutting LLM cost and latency**

## Selected Work

- **Multi-agent customer support (e-commerce/D2C):** LangGraph agents call order, returns and catalog APIs. They handle ~100K conversations a month and resolve ~55% of tickets on their own, saving ~$350K a year.
- **Conversational commerce assistant:** RAG over 50K+ SKUs with hybrid search and re-ranking. Chat-assisted conversion rose ~12%, adding ~$250K in annual revenue.
- **Policy and claims assistant (insurance/BFSI):** RAG over 20K+ documents with citations and PII guardrails. Average handling time dropped 40% for 150+ service agents.
- **Evals and cost:** CI-gated eval harnesses raised answer accuracy from ~78% to 92%+ before go-live. Semantic caching and model routing cut LLM spend by ~40% and p95 latency by ~35%.

## Featured Projects

- [Enterprise IT Support Agentic RAG Copilot](https://github.com/Anamitra-droid/Enterprise-IT-Support-Agentic-RAG-Copilot): Answers from a private Pinecone knowledge base, grades its evidence, and falls back to Tavily web search only when needed. FastAPI + LangGraph.
- [Secure Research Agent](https://github.com/Anamitra-droid/secure-research-agent): Multi-agent research system on AWS with LangGraph, Bedrock Guardrails, Redis caching and pgvector memory. Tested with LLM-as-a-Judge evals and PyRIT red-teaming.
- [Enterprise Research Agent](https://github.com/Anamitra-droid/enterprise-research-agent): LangGraph RAG plus web search with citations, conflict detection, clarifying questions and human-in-the-loop approvals.
- [Semiconductor Reliability Lifetime Analysis](https://github.com/Anamitra-droid/semiconductor-reliability-lifetime-analysis): Kaplan–Meier survival analysis, Weibull-2P modeling and Arrhenius acceleration on accelerated life test data.
- [HfO2 Edge ML Reliability](https://github.com/Anamitra-droid/hfO2-edge-ml-reliability): Physics-informed ML prototype that flags early degradation in HfO2 gate dielectrics with edge anomaly detection.
